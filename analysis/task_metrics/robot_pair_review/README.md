# Robot–robot contact review

All 40 recordings were searched using the saved 5 Hz trajectories. Robot pairs within a broad 0.85 m marker distance are review candidates, not collisions. Near observations separated by more than 1 s form separate episodes. The closest marker-distance timestamp from each episode was exported from the ORIGINAL video. This produced 257 encounter frames. All 257 frames were visually inspected: no robot–robot physical contact was confirmed. Before/after frames at approximately ±0.6 s were additionally inspected for the 19 closest encounters (minimum distances at most 0.60 m). Human handling, completed robots, and proximity alone are excluded.

The closest marker distance is 0.514 m at 70.2 s in 2026-09-13 16-49-26_llm.mp4, between robots 0 and 1; the original frames show a gap between bodies. Other close passes show avoidance or separation. Zero confirmed contacts in this review is not a sensor-derived guarantee of zero collisions: trajectories are sampled, positions can be missing, and only selected frames were reviewed rather than every frame throughout every encounter. Marker-distance minima also need not coincide with body-distance minima when robots rotate. Full collision totals remain unvalidated.

candidate_*.png show closest approaches; page_*.jpg collect these images in ascending marker-distance order. temporal_*.png and temporal_page_*.jpg show before/closest/after frames for the 19 closest passes. robot_pair_reviews.csv records review decisions; robot_robot_trial_review.csv gives per-recording confirmed observations and review scope. The four formerly uncertain pillar approaches were rejected explicitly by the user and are not counted.
