Exploration

P1: Explore the environment continuously to maximize area coverage while avoiding obstacles. 

P2: As a member of a team of three robots, explore the environment and avoid obstacles. 

P3: Explore the area while maximizing the coverage. 

P4: Explore the area. 

P5: Maximize the covered area. 

Patrolling

P1: Patrol the red, green, and blue zones in that order, while avoiding obstacles. 

P2: Each robot should visit red, green, and blue zones in that order. The robots should also avoid collisions.

P3: First go to the red, then to the green, and lastly to the blue zones. 

P4: Visit red, green, and blue locations. 

P5: Go to the red, green, and blue regions. 


Delivery

P1: Claim a box that is not claimed by another robot, approach it, and pick it up. Then search for the delivery zone that is the same color as the claimed box, approach it, and drop the box there. Each robot should deliver only one box and avoid the obstacles. 

P2: Using three robots, find the red, green, and blue boxes, deliver each box to its matching colored zone, and complete all three deliveries. Each robot should deliver one box and avoid collisions. 

P3: Choose an object, pick it up, and deliver it to its matching delivery zone. To pick up the object, the robot must search for and approach it. To drop the object, search for and approach the matching delivery zone. 

P4: There are three robots, three objects, and three delivery zones in the environment. Choose an object and deliver it to its matching zone. Each robot should deliver one object. 

P5: Each robot should collect a box and take it to its corresponding matching colored dropping zone. 


Complex Task

P1: Claim a color that is not claimed by another robot, approach its boxes, and execute one pickup to load a batch. One pickup loads one red, up to two green, or up to three blue boxes. Then search for the matching delivery zone, approach it, and execute one drop to unload the batch. Repeat the unchanged pickup/drop cycle until all three boxes of the claimed color are delivered. Each robot should complete one color and avoid obstacles.

P2: Using the existing events and unchanged plants, coordinate the robots to deliver three boxes per color. Treat one pickup as loading at most one red box, two green boxes, or three blue boxes, and one drop as unloading that batch at the matching zone.

P3: Complete a nine-box team delivery mission with three red, three green, and three blue boxes. With the plants unchanged, one pickup loads a batch of one red, up to two green, or up to three blue boxes; one drop unloads that batch. Retain the fixed safety specifications.

P4: Collect and deliver all three boxes of every color without adding or changing events or behavior plants. Interpret one pickup event as loading a batch of one red, up to two green, or up to three blue boxes before visiting the corresponding delivery zone.

P5: Deliver nine boxes to matching colored zones with unchanged plants. One pickup loads a batch limited to one red, two green, or three blue boxes, and one drop unloads it. Coordinate claims and completed colors using only the supplied event alphabet and fixed collision avoidance.
