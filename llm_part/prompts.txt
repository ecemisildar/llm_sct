Exploration 

P1: Move within the environment to maximize the area covered while avoiding obstacles.

P2: As a member of a team of robots, explore the area while not colliding with obstacles (i.e., walls or other robots).

P3: Move to maximize the area covered.

P4: As a member of a team, explore the area. 

P5:  Explore the environment to gain points: Receive 1 point per square meter visited. Lose -1 point for every obstacle you collide with. 

P6: As a member of a team of robots, explore the environment while collecting objects (i.e., touching them).

Patrolling

P1:Search for and visit the red, green, and blue locations in that order, while avoiding obstacles. If you reach a location out of sequence, continue your search for the correct location.

P2: As a member of a team of robots, go to the red, green, and blue regions precisely in that order. Avoid collisions with obstacles (i.e., walls or other robots).  If you reach a location out of sequence, continue your search for the correct location.

P3: Visit the red, green, and blue locations.

P4: As a member of a team, go to the red, green, and blue regions precisely in that order. 

P5: Visit the colored locations in any order. 

P6: As a team, visit the blue, green, and red regions as fast as possible.

Delivery

P1: Search for objects. Once an object has been detected, approach it, and, once it has been reached, pick it up and search for a suitable drop-off zone which could be the red zone, the blue zone, or both. Once a suitable zone has been detected, approach it. Once it has been reached, drop the object. This in turn automatically triggers a broadcast message that indicates the zone to which the object was delivered. Following delivery, resume with search to repeat the process indefinitely. At all times, keep track of the difference in the number of objects delivered to the red and blue zones. Choose which zones to deliver to such that the number of objects delivered to either differs always by -1 (i.e., the blue zone received one delivery more than the red zone), 0 (i.e., both zones received an equal amount of deliveries), or 1 (i.e., the red zone received one delivery more than the blue zone).

P2: As a member of a team of robots, search for an object and pick it up. Drop the object at a delivery zone. You can only drop it at a delivery zone if the last object was not delivered there. After the delivery, inform the other members about the successful delivery location. Collect all the objects in the environment.

P3: Search an object and pick it up. Search for a drop-off zone which could be the red zone, the blue zone, or both. Once reached, drop the object. This triggers a broadcast message that indicates the zone to which the object was delivered. Following delivery, repeat the process indefinitely. Choose which zones to deliver to such that the number of objects delivered to either differs always by -1, 0, or 1.

P4: As a member of a team, pick up an object and drop it at a delivery zone if the last object was not delivered there.

P5: Search an object and pick it up. Search for a drop-off zone which could be the red zone, the blue zone, or both. Once reached, drop the object. This triggers a broadcast message that indicates the zone to which the object was delivered. Following delivery, repeat the process indefinitely. Switch zones every time an object is delivered.

P6: As a team, collect all objects and drop them at the delivery zones. Do not deliver more than one object. Collect all the objects in the environment.
